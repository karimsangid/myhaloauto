Does My Halo Auto work on fleet vehicles?

Why On-Site Service Cuts Downtime

What fleet managers get when the mechanic comes to the vehicles instead of the vehicles going to a shop.

  • Less downtime

    No driving vehicles to a shop, no waiting rooms, no shuttling drivers back and forth. We come to where your vehicles are parked.

  • Scheduled with your fleet manager

    Service is coordinated directly with the person managing your vehicles, Monday–Saturday 7AM–7PM, with Sunday by request.

  • One ASE-certified point of contact

    Your vehicles are handled by the same certified technician, who explains each repair before work begins.

  • Itemized invoice per vehicle

    Each vehicle gets its own itemized invoice, so you have a clear record of the work performed and its cost.

How Fleet Service Works

A simple process from first message to finished work, with you approving each repair before it starts.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your vehicles

    Call, text or send the form with the year, make and model of each vehicle and what it needs.

  2. 2

    Get a transparent quote

    No hidden fees and no surprises. We explain every repair before we start, and you approve the work.

  3. 3

    Schedule with your fleet manager

    We set a time and place that works for your operation, Monday–Saturday 7AM–7PM or Sunday by request.

  4. 4

    On-site service, itemized invoice

    Work is performed at your lot, yard or job site, with an itemized invoice for each vehicle.*

What We Need at Your Lot, Yard or Job Site

On-site service requires adequate, safe workspace, such as a flat, level area of your lot, yard or garage, where the technician can work safely.*

  • Flat, level area around the vehicle
  • Room to work safely around the vehicle
