Does My Halo Auto work on fleet vehicles?

Yes. My Halo Auto provides mobile maintenance and repair for company and fleet vehicles, including work vans, pickups, sedans and SUVs, throughout DC, Maryland and Virginia. An ASE-certified technician performs the work at your location, scheduled with your fleet manager, with an itemized invoice for each vehicle.

Built for businesses and the fleet managers who keep their company vehicles on the road. You get the same service catalog, the same standards and the same technician we provide for personal vehicles, brought to where your vehicles are parked, so they spend less time off the road.
