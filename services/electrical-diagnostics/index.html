Can Electrical Diagnostics Be Done at My Location?

Yes. Electrical diagnostics and repairs are performed on-site at your home, office, lot or job site when there's adequate, safe workspace.

Mobile service requires adequate workspace and a suitable area to work safely, such as a flat driveway, parking lot or garage.*

Common Signs You May Need Electrical Diagnostics

These are common signs, not a diagnosis. The cause is confirmed with a hands-on diagnosis before any repair is recommended.

  • Check engine light on or flashing
  • Vehicle won't start or cranks slowly
  • Dim or flickering lights
  • Battery warning light
  • Intermittent electrical problems
