Mobile Oil Changes & General Maintenance in DC, Maryland & Virginia

My Halo Auto provides mobile oil changes and routine maintenance across DC, Maryland and Virginia. An ASE-certified technician changes oil and filters, replaces spark plugs, flushes coolant and other fluids, and replaces belts and hoses at your home, office or fleet lot.

What Does Mobile Maintenance Include?

Oil changes, fluid flushes, filters, spark plugs, and complete tune-ups.

  • Oil change & filter

    Old engine oil drained, a new oil filter installed and the engine refilled with fresh oil.

  • Spark plug replacement

    Worn spark plugs replaced and torqued to manufacturer specification.

  • Coolant & fluid flush

    Old coolant and other worn-out fluids flushed and refilled.

  • Belt & hose replacement

    Worn, cracked or glazed belts and hoses replaced.

Can Maintenance Be Done at My Location?

Yes. Routine maintenance is performed on-site at your home, office, lot or job site when there's adequate, safe workspace.

Available across Washington, DC (all four quadrants), Maryland (Silver Spring, Bethesda, Rockville, College Park, Bowie, Laurel and more) and Northern Virginia (Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, Reston, Tysons, Manassas and more).

Mobile service requires adequate workspace and a suitable area to work safely, such as a flat driveway, parking lot or garage.*

When to Schedule Maintenance

These are common reasons to book routine maintenance. If something points to a repair, the cause is confirmed with a hands-on diagnosis before any work is recommended.

  • Oil change reminder or mileage due
  • Rough idle or reduced fuel economy
  • Squealing belts
  • Cracked or soft hoses
  • Fluids that look dark or low
