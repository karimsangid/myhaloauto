What Does Mobile Brake Service Include?

Complete brake system service from pad replacement to full brake overhauls.

  • Brake pad & rotor replacement

    Worn pads and rotors replaced, with every fastener torqued to manufacturer specification.

  • Caliper service & rebuild

    Sticking or leaking calipers serviced or rebuilt.

  • Brake line repair

    Damaged or corroded brake lines repaired.

  • Hub & bearing replacement

    Worn wheel hubs and bearings replaced.

Can Brake Service Be Done at My Location?

Yes. Brake service is performed on-site at your home, office, lot or job site when there's adequate, safe workspace, such as a flat driveway, parking lot or garage.

Available across Washington, DC (all four quadrants), Maryland (Silver Spring, Bethesda, Rockville, College Park, Bowie, Laurel and more) and Northern Virginia (Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, Reston, Tysons, Manassas and more).

Mobile service requires adequate workspace and a suitable area to work safely, such as a flat driveway, parking lot or garage.*

Common Signs You May Need Brake Service

These are common signs, not a diagnosis. The cause is confirmed with a hands-on diagnosis before any repair is recommended.

  • Squealing or grinding when braking
  • A pulsing or soft brake pedal
  • The vehicle pulls to one side when braking
  • Brake warning light
  • Humming or grinding from a wheel that changes with speed (hub or bearing)

Not sure? Describe what you're noticing when you request a quote.
