Common signs

Common Signs You May Need Brake Service

These are common signs, not a diagnosis. The cause is confirmed with a hands-on diagnosis before any repair is recommended.

  • Squealing or grinding when braking
  • A pulsing or soft brake pedal
  • The vehicle pulls to one side when braking
  • Brake warning light
  • Humming or grinding from a wheel that changes with speed (hub or bearing)
