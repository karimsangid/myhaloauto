What's Included

Complete engine diagnostics, repair, and rebuilds. From misfires and oil leaks to full overhauls.

  • Full engine diagnostics

    We trace running problems such as misfires, rough idle and warning lights back to their cause before recommending a repair.

  • Timing belt/chain replacement

    Replacement of worn timing components, installed and torqued to manufacturer specification.

  • Head gasket repair

    Diagnosis and repair of head gasket failures.

  • Oil leak diagnosis & repair

    We find where the oil is coming from and repair the leaking gasket or seal.

Can Engine Repair Be Done at My Location?

Engine diagnostics are performed on-site. Whether a larger engine job, such as timing work or a head gasket, can be done at your location depends on the job and the workspace available. Describe the job when you request a quote and we'll work out the best arrangement. Engine swaps are never a mobile service; see engine swaps*.

Mobile service requires adequate workspace and a suitable area to work safely, such as a flat driveway, parking lot or garage.*

Common Signs You May Need Engine Repair

These are common signs, not a diagnosis. The cause is confirmed with a hands-on diagnosis before any repair is recommended.

  • Check engine light is on
  • Misfires or rough idle
  • Oil spots under the vehicle
  • Overheating or coolant loss
  • Unusual engine noises

Not sure? Describe what you're noticing when you request a quote.
